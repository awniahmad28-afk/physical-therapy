Questions

Good to know

Do I need to swim?

No. The pool is 1.2 m deep and every exercise is done standing or supported.

What should I bring?

A swimsuit and towel. We provide changing rooms, showers and lockers.

Is hydrotherapy covered by insurance?

Most private insurers cover it as part of a physiotherapy plan. We'll check for you.
