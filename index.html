Adding your own photos and videos

Every striped box is a slot like this. The placeholder disappears as soon as you fill in src:

<figure class="media" data-label="TREATMENT IMAGE 1250 × 1000 · 5:4">
  <img src="../../media/manual-therapy.jpg" alt="Therapist mobilising a patient's shoulder">
</figure>
  1. Put your files in the media/ folder at the top of the project.
  2. Open the design file and search for MEDIA ZONE or src="".
  3. In designs/elegant/, paths start with ../../media/. The video heroes currently play a demo film of soft window light from media/demo/. Replace it with your own footage: an MP4 alone is enough.
  4. For a video, set its <source src> and its poster (a still image shown while it loads). Background films should stay muted autoplay loop playsinline. Visitors who have asked their device for reduced motion see the video paused and the page without animation.
  5. Any image slot can hold a video instead: replace <img> with <video> inside the same <figure>.

Export photos at the listed size as JPG or WebP, under about 300 KB. Keep videos as MP4 under 10 MB. For a consistent, upmarket look, use one photographer and one lighting style across the whole site.

Which one suits you

MaisonA traditional private practice or a clinic with older, affluent patients who value formality and reassurance.
NocturneAn exclusive or members-style practice, sports medicine for executives, or evening and city-centre clinics.
LinenA boutique studio offering physio and Pilates, women's health, or a neighbourhood practice that wants to feel warm but premium.
AtelierA design-led brand with strong photography that wants the boldest editorial look.
Video or scroll?Choose a video hero if you can film your clinic. It shows the space and people at once. Choose scroll animation if you have great still photos: it makes every section feel crafted.
