Adding your own photos and videos

Every striped box is a slot like this. The placeholder disappears as soon as you fill in src:

<figure class="media" data-label="TREATMENT IMAGE 1250 × 1000 · 5:4">
  <img src="../../media/manual-therapy.jpg" alt="Therapist mobilising a patient's shoulder">
</figure>
  1. Put your files in the media/ folder at the top of the project.
  2. Open the design file and search for MEDIA ZONE or src="".
  3. For the new designs in designs/elegant/, use paths starting with ../../media/. For Atelier, use ../media/.
  4. For a video, set both src and poster (a still image shown while it loads). Background films should stay muted autoplay loop playsinline.
  5. Any image slot can hold a video instead: replace <img> with <video> inside the same <figure>.

Export photos at the listed size as JPG or WebP, under about 300 KB. Keep videos as MP4 under 10 MB. For a consistent, upmarket look, use one photographer and one lighting style across the whole site.
