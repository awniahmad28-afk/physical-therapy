Red light therapy

Recover in the red light room

Our full-body Optimal Health red light bed sits in its own calm, private room. Sessions can be booked on their own or alongside your physiotherapy.

Questions

Good to know

Can red light therapy be part of my physiotherapy?

Yes, it can be added to your plan. Your physiotherapist will advise whether it is suitable for you.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Come as you are. Our team will explain the session and answer your questions before you start.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on your goals. We will recommend a plan after your consultation.
